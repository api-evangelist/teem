Real estate leaders are surrounded by workplace data, but the biggest challenge is knowing what to do with it. As hybrid work patterns continue to evolve, portfolio decisions require more than utilization metrics and benchmark statistics. This webinar explores how organizations can improve data quality, connect workplace systems, uncover the story behind the numbers, and use scenario planning to turn real estate insights into measurable business outcomes.
